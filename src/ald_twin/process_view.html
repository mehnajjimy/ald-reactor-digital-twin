Scope and acceptance rules

Only the declared recipes and named scenarios are covered. An uncomputed point has no established feasibility.

A completion must reach 0.90, B remaining must be at most 0.10, and both scaled purge residuals must be at most 0.01. Cases also need numerical verification and the declared wall-transport screen. Near-threshold results stay unresolved.

Selections minimize prescribed cycle time and precursor delivery over the finite candidate list. Selected recipes receive an additional spatial and time check. Relative GPC spread is (maximum − minimum) / mean.

Source hashes, original cycles and failed attempts remain in the study records. This view does not run a different solver or estimate missing properties.
